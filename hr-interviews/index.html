HR Interview Manager

Track candidates through 3 interview steps and balance the load across interviewers.

Candidates

Add a candidate, then set a date and an interviewer for each step. The dropdown shows each interviewer's current load so you can pick the least-busy person.

Interviewers

The team roster. Add everyone who conducts interviews.

Cumulative interview load

Total interviews assigned to each person, all candidates and steps combined. Hover a bar for the breakdown.

Data is saved automatically in this browser (localStorage). Use Export backup to keep a copy or move to another computer.
